Operating principles

Consultative selling, the way I practice it

Fight unnecessary complexity, put the client at the center, co-create so people defend what they helped build, and earn the right to pitch by being useful first. Revenue follows relevance.

Relationship pipeline

Buyer psychology and the warm-path reality

Influencers open doors that decision-makers keep shut, most buyers are not in market when you reach them, and a warm introduction still converts several times better than a cold one. The system is built around that reality rather than against it.
