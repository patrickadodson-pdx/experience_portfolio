Business Development & GTM · Professional services · Relationship-led, AI-native

Relationship-led growth, carried by AI and data.

Business development used to scale on effort. Show up enough, and the network renewed itself: rooms, hallway conversations, the conference circuit, clients who got promoted and brought you along. Then the flow of opportunities slowed, a little at first, and now the shortfall is obvious. Buyers arrive late with their minds mostly made up, and if you do not have a relationship inside the account, you are pitching against someone who does.

The best work in professional services still arrives through people and effort. What changed is that AI, data, and automation can now carry the research, timing, and coverage load that used to cap how many relationships one person could find, nurture, and convert. I build the system that puts the right practitioners in front of the right prospect at the right moment, with a real reason to engage, and keeps the relationship human at the point of contact.

The doer-seller model rested on a bet that the network would keep renewing itself through sheer presence. That bet is failing. Buyers self-educate, arrive late, and decide early, and the seller with an existing relationship inside the account wins before the formal process starts. Effort alone does not fix a coverage and timing problem, and adding headcount just multiplies the same manual limits.

The method

A practice built over two decades, with an AI execution layer built over the last two years.

The consultative discipline is not new. The way it now runs is.

Operating principles

Consultative selling, the way I practice it

Fight unnecessary complexity, put the client at the center, co-create so people defend what they helped build, and earn the right to pitch by being useful first. Revenue follows relevance.

Relationship pipeline

Buyer psychology and the warm-path reality

Influencers open doors that decision-makers keep shut, most buyers are not in market when you reach them, and a warm introduction still converts several times better than a cold one. The system is built around that reality rather than against it.

Execution layer

How I would run it

Signal detection, enrichment, scoring, and prospect dossiers that used to take days to compile are now produced in hours, aligning a team for strategic pursuit against the accounts and moments that actually matter.
