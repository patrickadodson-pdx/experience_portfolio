AI Adoption & Change · ICANN, 2012 to 2018

Six years helping ICANN through change.

Strategy activation, facilitation, and information design with ICANN's staff, Board, and global multistakeholder community, through a new CEO, a new strategic plan, the transition of IANA stewardship, and the new bylaws that followed it.

From late 2012 to 2016, XPLANE worked alongside ICANN through one of the most consequential stretches in its history. I led the account, grew it to more than $1M a year in billings, and worked on the delivery team. The work below is the team's, collected from the executive ebook we produced for ICANN in 2016. In late 2016 I joined ICANN as a Senior Strategic Program Manager, and the final section is work I led from inside the organization.

Much of it was deliberately unfinished when it first met its audience: napkin sketches and blueprint-level drafts that invited the community to shape the result rather than judge it. That is how the maps earned trust across groups with competing interests.

"We count on this partnership to help us navigate along the path of change." Fadi Chehadé, ICANN CEO

Late 2012 to 2013

Aligning a community around new leadership

One World, One Internet

ICANN's incoming CEO, Fadi Chehadé, wanted clear visual tools to introduce himself to a community spanning governments, business, and technical experts. We built two: an infographic of the internet governance ecosystem and a map of ICANN's own ecosystem, developed through a phased, iterative process with stakeholder groups reviewing at every step. A re-rendered version has been in use since 2015.

Who Runs the Internet?

Built from low-fidelity napkin sketches so the wider ecosystem could shape how it was represented before anything was finished. ICANN chose to keep the hand-drawn version to signal that the ecosystem is still evolving, and it was featured on Wikipedia's internet governance article from January 2013.

Culture Change Initiative

One-on-one interviews with key executives and board members, then a one-day workshop with selected staff, synthesized against the CEO's goals into a 30-page Culture Assessment and Change report. Paired maps showed current and future structure with responsibilities and friction points, and every recommendation carried its own case, goals, obstacles, and actionable tasks.

2013 to 2016

Making complex work legible

Security Map

One of the first internal teams to ask for visual support: a map of the security team's scope of services and its role in coordinating stakeholders around security events.

Domain Name Industry

A loosely organized industry that needed to organize more effectively. A facilitated session in New York City produced the visual frameworks the group aligned around.

ASO Map

A one-day design sprint with former IANA General Manager Barbara Roseman produced a napkin-level sketch of the numbers policy life cycle. She socialized it over several months, ending in a working session with the Numbers Community at ICANN49 in Singapore.

Internet Governance

Frameworks, sketches, and prototypes that helped a panel of industry and government leaders explore what 21st century governance of the internet could look like.

Public Responsibility

A map for the new Development and Public Responsibility Department showing global newcomers where they can engage, first in 2014 and updated in 2016 as the programs grew.

Accountability Mechanisms

Working with ICANN's legal team to map key accountability use cases to the mechanisms that address them, so the community could see when it had standing and which mechanism to use.

The Root Zone

An infographic explaining how the root zone is managed, maintained, and secured, previewed with stakeholder groups at ICANN50 in London for accuracy before publication on icann.org. Precise enough for technical experts, readable for newcomers.

2014 to 2015

Brand for a new division

GDD Brand Foundation and Visual Identity Guidelines

A brand strategy engagement for the newly formed Generic Domains Division: a two-day workshop in Los Angeles co-created its Brand Foundation, covering essence, audience, story, values, personality, and tone. A companion effort set visual identity guidelines for the ICANN master brand.

2013 to 2015

Moving groups to decisions

Meetings Strategy Working Group

A Board-created multistakeholder working group, a year in and stalled on how ICANN's meetings should evolve. Workshops, executive interviews, and community discussions across ICANN47, 48, and 49 led to a final report and a Board decision at ICANN50 in London.

Strategic Plan FY16 to FY20

A bottom-up, multilingual planning process that opened with a teaser animation at ICANN46 in Beijing, gathered public input across eight strategic areas through facilitated sessions, and ended with the Board adopting the plan in late 2014. We designed the interactive plan document, a four-minute narrative animation, and posters for ICANN52.

ICANN47 to ICANN54

Shaping the CEO's story

Opening ceremony keynotes

From Durban in 2013 through Dublin in 2015, we worked with the CEO and his communications team on every opening keynote, shaping the narrative and translating it into visuals. Much of it was built on site in the days before each ceremony, as events unfolded.

2014 to 2016

Through the IANA stewardship transition

IANA Stewardship Transition

The most significant change in ICANN's history: the US government's oversight role would pass to the global community. Working groups had to design what would replace it, and for the Names community the stakes were highest.

Enhancing Accountability

In parallel, the CCWG-Accountability had to evolve existing mechanisms and define new ones. We facilitated working sessions in Istanbul, Paris, Los Angeles, and at ICANN meetings, and created visual summaries of each proposal so people could understand them without parsing long, dense documents.

Global Multistakeholder Community

A short presentation that equipped staff to explain who participates in the community, where, and how, mapping broad stakeholder categories into ICANN's supporting organizations and advisory committees.

ICANN Reviews

Information design to build understanding of, and participation in, the Organizational Reviews program, starting with a presentation for ICANN55 in Marrakech.

2015 to 2016

Explaining digital governance

The Three Layers of Digital Governance

Most people did not understand ICANN's limited role in internet governance, and scrutiny rose after the 2014 transition announcement. Synthesizing existing models, we worked with ICANN to describe digital governance in three layers: infrastructure, logical, and economic and societal. Detailed views of the logical layer, where ICANN sits, and of the economic and societal layer followed.

At ICANN, 2017 to 2018

Making governance usable

Empowered Community powers

The bylaws adopted with the IANA stewardship transition gave the community new powers to hold ICANN's Board and organization accountable, written in dense legal text few people could follow. As Program Lead for governance activation, I designed and facilitated in-person and virtual workshops that turned the bylaw language into step-by-step process maps, and refined them through iterative review until every stakeholder group was aligned. Published in October 2017 alongside text handbooks, they became the community's visual toolkit for exercising its powers: who acts, in what order, by when, and where a petition ends.

Process Documentation Initiative

The same approach, applied to how ICANN's core processes actually run. Starting in January 2017, I produced process flow maps for organizational and specific reviews, the GNSO, ccNSO, and ASO policy development processes, GAC and ALAC advice, Board advice, and correspondence, each showing roles, responsibilities, timeframes, and the points where confusion or impasse tends to arise. Drafts were reviewed with the community at ICANN59 in Johannesburg before publication with companion handbooks. The Board advice map shown is ICANN's 2023 revision of the original.
